Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) is a photo app with AI looks and on-device film looks that also includes 75 Calm, a 75-day routine and reflection challenge. It is operated by Stuke Digital LLC. You can use it without creating an account.

Your writing stays on your device

Your name or nickname, onboarding answers, start date, promise and signature, challenge progress, contact-boundary progress, journal entries, and unsent messages are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload this content to Stuke Digital LLC, RevenueCat, Supabase, or an AI provider. Your writing and signature are never used for analytics.

The app uses Apple's file protection for its private files and excludes its app-managed data folder from automatic device backups. Data is not synced between devices. Local data stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Photos stay on your device unless you create an AI look

Photo check-ins are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Progress stories, film looks, grain, light leaks, date stamps, and clear skin are made entirely on your device, and photos you use with them are not uploaded. Clear skin detects faces on your device only to retouch the photo you are editing; face data is not stored, shared, or used to identify you.

AI looks

AI looks are optional and part of Premium. Before your first AI look, the app explains how it works and asks for your consent. Nothing is sent unless you agree. Only the photo you choose for an AI look leaves your device: the app resizes it (up to 1280 pixels, as a JPEG) and sends it to our server, a Supabase Edge Function, which forwards it to OpenRouter. OpenRouter routes the request to Google Gemini or another model provider to create the edited image, which is returned to your device.

We do not keep your photos. Each photo is used only to create the result you requested, deleted right after processing, and never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models. To count your daily AI looks, our server uses a one-way hash of your App Store transaction identifier. To prevent abuse, it uses a one-way hash of the request's IP address for one hour for rate limiting; the IP address itself is not stored.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the AI looks switch in Me. If you decline or withdraw consent, the app does not send your photos, and film looks and the rest of the app keep working.

Subscriptions and purchase records

Apple processes purchases and payments. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. RevenueCat helps verify subscription access, restore purchases, and report subscription activity. It processes purchase records such as product identifiers, transaction and renewal information, subscription status, and an anonymous app-specific identifier. The app does not supply RevenueCat with your name, email, writing, signature, or onboarding answers.

Purchase records may be retained as needed to provide and restore subscription access, handle purchase support, and meet applicable obligations. Deleting local app data does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple's purchase records.

Public settings and service requests

The app uses Supabase to read public price-display settings and, when available, published community information. These requests do not contain your name, private writing, signature, contact details, or onboarding answers. Service providers process standard connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver and protect network requests. The app does not collect an advertising identifier, sell personal data, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

This release does not send native onboarding or journal activity to a behavioral analytics service. The only content processed by AI is a photo you choose for an AI look, after your consent, as described above.

Notifications and device access

Reminders are optional local notifications. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings. You choose photos with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select, and saving an image to Photos uses add-only access. The app does not access your address book or send messages to anyone. Drawing a promise is optional and stays local.

Export and deletion

In Me → Your data, you can export or delete your local app data. An export can contain private writing; it is shared only with the destination you choose. Deletion also removes exports still held by the app. Copies you already shared remain with their recipients or chosen storage services. Photos sent for AI looks are deleted from our server right after processing.

To cancel a subscription, use Apple subscription settings. Uninstalling the app or deleting its data does not cancel a subscription.

Support and your choices

For privacy questions, data requests, or support, email stukeleo@gmail.com. We process information you voluntarily send to answer your request and meet applicable obligations. Please do not send journal entries, unsent messages, signatures, or sensitive personal details.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information. Contact us to make a request. We cannot access or recover writing or photos that remain only on your device.

Service providers and policy changes

Apple, RevenueCat, and Supabase process information to provide their services and protect their systems. OpenRouter and the model provider it routes a request to, such as Google Gemini, process a photo you choose for an AI look only after your consent and only to create your result. Their own privacy policies also apply to that processing. We require service providers receiving data from our apps to provide protections consistent with this policy and applicable App Store requirements.

Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) is intended for adults. We may update this policy when the app's practices change, and will update the date above.
