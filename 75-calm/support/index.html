Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) Support

Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) is published by Stuke Digital LLC.

Contact support

For help with Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm), subscriptions, privacy, or app issues, email stukeleo@gmail.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, and a short description of the issue. Do not send journal entries, unsent messages, signatures, or sensitive personal details.

Premium is missing

Open Me or the Premium offer and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription. An internet connection is required to check purchase access. Cancelling a purchase attempt does not erase your plan or progress.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open Apple subscription settings to view renewal details or cancel. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. For refund requests, use Apple's purchase support.

Progress, writing, and a new phone

Your local plan, progress, journal, and unsent messages stay on your device. This release does not sync them across devices. Restoring a purchase restores eligible Premium access, not deleted local writing. A missed challenge day or contact-boundary event does not erase previously completed challenge days.

Export or delete your data

Open Me → Your data to export or delete local data. Deletion also removes exports still held by the app. Copies already shared elsewhere remain in the destination you chose.

Privacy and terms

When you need immediate support

Glow Studio (formerly 75 Calm) provides photo tools and, in its 75 Calm tab, a personal routine and reflection tools. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions, provide crisis monitoring, or guarantee recovery. This support inbox is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or someone you trust.
